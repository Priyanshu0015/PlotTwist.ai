## 🌟 About The Project

While most AI writing tools just generate static blocks of text, **PlotTwist** acts as an interactive "Choose Your Own Adventure" engine. Powered by the Google Gemini API, it serves as an AI "Dungeon Master" that generates opening scenes and branching choices. Users can guide the plot by selecting AI-suggested paths or inputting their own wild twists to create endless, dynamic stories.

Built specifically for the AI Tools Hackathon under the **Storytelling and Narrative Assistants** track.

### ✨ Key Features
- **Dynamic Branching Narratives:** The AI offers 3 contextual choices after every scene to drive the plot forward.
- **Collaborative Flow:** Accept AI suggestions or override them with your own creative directions.
- **Context-Aware Memory:** The application remembers previous choices, ensuring a consistent and logical story progression.
- **Sleek UI:** A responsive, dark-mode focused reading and interaction interface.
